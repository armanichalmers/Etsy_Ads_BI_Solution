# 🛍️ Etsy Ads Business Intelligence Solution

## 📌 Project Overview

This capstone project presents a **data-informed Business Intelligence solution for Etsy Ads** designed to address gaps in data transparency, real-time reporting, seller insights, and advertising performance.

The project evaluates Etsy's business environment and advertising challenges before proposing a centralized BI solution that integrates advertising data, seller performance, customer behavior, and external market trends.

The proposed strategy incorporates **real-time reporting, seller-facing dashboards, predictive analytics, A/B testing, automated recommendations, cloud data infrastructure, and data governance** to help Etsy and its sellers make faster, more informed decisions.

Supporting data visualizations were developed in **Microsoft Power BI** throughout the program and incorporated into the capstone analysis and presentation.

---

## 🎯 Business Challenge

Etsy Ads plays an important role in helping sellers promote their products, but gaps in real-time insights, data transparency, campaign guidance, and customization can make it difficult for sellers to understand and optimize advertising performance.

The project explores how Etsy could use Business Intelligence to:

- Improve transparency into advertising performance
- Provide sellers with more actionable campaign insights
- Support faster, data-driven decision-making
- Improve advertising efficiency and seller engagement
- Strengthen seller trust and retention
- Support long-term advertising revenue growth
- Strengthen Etsy's competitive position within e-commerce

---

## 💡 Proposed Business Intelligence Solution

The proposed solution centers on a **centralized, cloud-based Business Intelligence environment** that brings together Etsy Ads data, seller performance, customer behavior, sales activity, and external market trends.

Key components of the proposed solution include:

- **Centralized Data Integration** — consolidating advertising, seller, customer, sales, and external market data into a unified environment.
- **Seller-Facing Dashboards** — providing sellers with clear insights into campaign performance, audience activity, and advertising efficiency.
- **Predictive Analytics** — using historical data and analytical models to support bidding, targeting, forecasting, and campaign optimization.
- **A/B Testing** — enabling sellers to evaluate campaign variations and identify strategies that improve performance.
- **Competitive & Customer Intelligence** — incorporating market and customer trends to support more informed advertising decisions.
- **Automated Reporting & Alerts** — providing timely performance updates and identifying significant changes that may require action.
- **Data Governance & Security** — establishing controls for data quality, privacy, access, and regulatory compliance.

The proposed system is designed to improve both **seller decision-making and Etsy's internal advertising operations** while creating a scalable foundation for future analytics capabilities.

## 🔎 Strategic Analysis

The project incorporated several business and analytical frameworks to evaluate Etsy's current environment and determine the requirements of the proposed BI solution.

### PESTEL Analysis

A PESTEL analysis evaluated external factors affecting Etsy and its advertising ecosystem, including:

- Political and advertising regulations
- Economic conditions and consumer spending
- Social and sustainability trends
- AI, machine learning, and mobile-commerce developments
- Environmental considerations
- Legal, privacy, and intellectual-property requirements

### SWOT Analysis

A SWOT analysis evaluated Etsy's internal position and external environment, including:

- Brand identity and seller community
- Advertising transparency and customization limitations
- Opportunities for AI-driven personalization and international growth
- Competitive pressure from other e-commerce platforms
- Changing consumer preferences and regulatory requirements

### Gap Analysis

The gap analysis compared Etsy Ads' existing capabilities with the capabilities required for the proposed future-state BI environment.

Areas evaluated included:

- Real-time data availability
- External market-data integration
- Advertising campaign optimization
- Seller dashboard functionality
- Advertising performance feedback

## ⚙️ Implementation Strategy

A phased implementation roadmap was developed to transition from the current environment to the proposed BI solution.

### Proposed Implementation Phases

1. **Set Up the BI Platform and Data Warehouse**
2. **Connect Data Sources Through ETL Integration**
3. **Develop Seller-Facing Dashboards and Predictive Tools**
4. **Train Internal Teams and Sellers**
5. **Establish Security and Governance Protocols**
6. **Launch, Monitor, and Iterate**

The proposed implementation spans approximately **10 months**, beginning with infrastructure development and data integration and progressing through dashboard development, training, governance, pilot deployment, and ongoing optimization.

## 💰 Projected Business Impact

A financial assessment was developed to evaluate the potential value of implementing the proposed BI solution.

### Estimated Investment

**Three-Year Investment: $3.18M**

The proposed investment includes:

- BI software
- Cloud infrastructure
- Data warehousing
- ETL and data integration
- Dashboard development
- Predictive analytics and AI tools
- Training and seller onboarding
- Data governance and security
- System maintenance and support
- Change management

### Projected Benefits

**Estimated Annual Benefits: $4.7M**

Projected benefits include:

- **$1.0M** from increased seller retention
- **$1.2M** from improved ad conversion rates
- **$1.5M** from advertising revenue growth
- **$400K** in operational cost savings
- **$600K** from reduced seller churn

The solution plan estimates an **annual ROI of 47.8%** and an **adjusted three-year ROI of approximately 84%**.

Additional anticipated benefits include increased seller trust, improved data transparency, faster decision-making, stronger cross-functional collaboration, and greater platform engagement.

## 🎓 Project Background

This project was completed as the **capstone project for my Master of Science in Business Intelligence program**.

Rather than developing a single dashboard, the capstone focused on designing a comprehensive **data-informed Business Intelligence solution** for a real-world business challenge. Supporting visualizations developed in **Microsoft Power BI** throughout the program contributed to the analysis and were incorporated into the final capstone presentation.

The project brought together **business research, strategic analysis, data visualization, BI solution design, data management planning, analytics strategy, implementation planning, financial evaluation, and executive communication**.

The final deliverables demonstrate the ability to move from **business problem identification to a proposed enterprise BI strategy**, while considering the technology, data, people, processes, governance, costs, and expected business value required to support implementation.
